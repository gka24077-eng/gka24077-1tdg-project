Join the Writing Club

Ready to Join? Interested in writing, being creative, or meeting other students who enjoy the same things? We'd love to have you join the Writing Club! You don't need to be an experienced writer. Whether you love creating stories, writing poetry, or just want to try something different, everyone is welcome

📅 Club Meetings

Day: Every Thursday Time: 1:30 PM – 2:10 PM Location: H24 Our Writing Club meets every Thursday during lunch. Come along, bring your ideas, and spend your lunch doing something creative!

How to Join

Joining is super easy! Just fill out the sign-up form below with some basic information about yourself and your writing interests. The form helps us understand what students are interested in so we can plan activities, writing challenges, and events that everyone can enjoy. Join the Writing Club

What Happens After You Sign Up?

Once you've completed the form, you'll be able to come along to our next meeting and get involved! You can meet other members, take part in writing activities, work on your own ideas, and have fun while improving your writing.
